Effort zones

  • Z1
    Recovery · RPE 1–2

    Very easy. You could hold a full conversation. Warm-ups, cool-downs, recovery spins.

  • Z2
    Endurance · RPE 3–4

    Easy and sustainable. You can talk in full sentences. Most of your training lives here.

  • Z3
    Tempo, race effort · RPE 5–6

    Steady and focused. Short sentences only. This is roughly your 70.3 bike and run effort.

  • Z4
    Threshold · RPE 7–8

    Hard. A few words at a time. Intervals only, never the whole session.

  • Z5
    Max · RPE 9–10

    Very hard. Only in short strides and pickups.

Ground rules

  • Each day, tap Trained or Didn’t train. Add time and distance from your watch if you like. Times can be minutes (45) or h:mm (1:05).
  • Need something different? Tap Change workout to turn any session into a swim, bike, run, strength workout or hike, or pick a travel option like bands or a hotel-gym bike. Tap Move to swap two days in a week.
  • Missed a workout? Let it go. Don't stack it onto the next day.
  • Sick, or pain that changes how you move? Take the day off.
  • Recovery weeks are planned lighter on purpose. Keep them light.
  • Indoor trainer rides and treadmill runs count.
  • From Build onward, fuel rides over 90 minutes with 60–80 g of carbs per hour.
  • Swim distances are in yards. Your race swim is 2,112 yd.

Changes save to your team and sync to every phone. They’re kept on this phone while you’re offline.
